ABOUT ME

From Business Engineering
to Financial Markets

I am Salim El Mallem, a Business Engineering student at Solvay Brussels School of Economics and Management, specializing in Financial Engineering and currently studying at Université Paris Dauphine – PSL as part of the QTEM program.

Throughout my studies, I developed a strong interest in the intersection of finance, mathematics and programming. This progressively led me towards financial markets, particularly derivatives, trading and structuring.

My experience at BNP Paribas Asset Management gave me practical exposure to the trade lifecycle across equities, fixed income, ETFs and derivatives, complementing the quantitative perspective developed through my academic studies.

Today, I continue to deepen this interest through financial engineering projects focused on derivatives pricing, interest-rate modelling, volatility and risk management. This portfolio brings these projects together and documents both their quantitative implementation and the financial intuition behind them.
